Ask a question
Does it cost anything to work with you?

No. Your consultation is free, and you never pay a fee for my guidance. Agents are typically compensated by the insurance carrier when you enroll, and working with an advisor doesn’t add to the price of your plan.

Do you speak Spanish?

Yes. I’m bilingual and happy to work with you in English or Spanish. Use the EN / ES switch at the top of the page to read this site in either language, and just tell me which you prefer when we talk.

What should I have ready for our call?

It helps to know your state, who needs coverage, your doctors and any prescriptions you take, and your estimated household income if you’re shopping Marketplace plans. Don’t worry if you don’t have everything. We’ll sort it out together.

Can I keep my doctors and medications?

That’s the first thing we check. Every plan has its own provider network and drug list (called a formulary), so I compare your doctors and prescriptions against each plan you’re considering before you choose.

When can I enroll in a plan?

Most people enroll during Open Enrollment, which for Marketplace plans begins Nov 1. Outside that window you may still qualify for a Special Enrollment Period after a life event such as losing coverage, moving, getting married or having a baby, and small businesses can often start group coverage outside Open Enrollment. Book a call and I’ll tell you which window applies to you.

I lost my coverage or my situation changed. Now what?

Reach out as soon as it happens. A job change, a move, marriage, a new baby or losing coverage can open a Special Enrollment Period, typically about 60 days from the event, and I’ll make sure we don’t miss your window.

Can you help my small business?

Yes. I help owners understand their options, from small teams to owner-only setups, and what fits their budget. Book a call and we’ll look at what makes sense for your business.
